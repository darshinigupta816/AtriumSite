About

Moments that bring people together

Welcome to Atrium Pointe, a refined and versatile venue curated to cultivate connection, inspire collaboration, and honor life’s most meaningful milestones. We believe every get together tells a distinct story. Whether you are hosting a private event, a milestone occasion, corporate or social workshops, our aim is to provide an exquisite, adaptable environment that elevates your occasion into an unforgettable memory.

Our venue serves as a customizable space, seamlessly adapting to match your unique vision for everything from elegant galas to intimate gatherings.

FAQ

Booking and Logistics

What is your deposit and payment schedule?

$300 deposit refundable after the event. 50% payment at the time of booking. 50% payment a week before the event.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellation allowed 2 weeks prior to the event date.

Do you have onsite parking?

Yes, we have ample well-lit parking.

What is your cleaning policy?

We charge $100 for cleaning.

What happens if my event runs over?

We understand that events can run behind schedule. If you need to extend your booking, extended hours are subject to availability and additional charges will apply.

What hours are included in the rental charges?

Specific setup, event, and tear-down time.

What is your maximum guest capacity?

60 people.

Do I need event insurance?

Yes, you will need one-day event insurance. We can provide you with more details at the time of booking.

What tables, chairs, and equipment are included?

Our homepage has all the details under the Venue tab.

Is the space wheelchair accessible?

Confirmed ADA compliance bathroom and ramp access.

Are there any restrictions on decor?

No hanging anything from the ceiling, no nails, and no glue on walls and floor.

Do you have a preferred vendor list?

Yes, we can provide upon request.

Can we bring our own decorators, caterers, and alcohol?

Yes, you can.
